Our Policies

Clear, fair, and puppy-first policies for your peace of mind

🩺 Health Guarantee

All puppies are health checked, fully vaccinated, dewormed, and examined by licensed veterinarians before delivery. 2-year genetic health warranty included.

🏠 Ethical Breeding

We partner only with ethical, licensed breeders who prioritize puppy health, temperament, and loving home environments. No puppy mills. Ever.

💰 Deposit & Reservation

A non-refundable deposit of 20% is required to reserve your chosen puppy. Full payment is due upon delivery or pickup. Deposits secure your puppy for 7 days.

🚚 Delivery Policy

Nationwide white-glove delivery in climate-controlled vehicles. Delivery timelines depend on your location and weather conditions. Free delivery within 50 miles.

💬 Lifetime Support

We provide ongoing support even after adoption. Our team is available 24/7 via WhatsApp and email for any questions about your puppy's health, training, or care.

🔄 7-Day Health Guarantee

If your puppy has any serious health issues within 7 days of delivery, we offer a full replacement or refund. Terms and conditions apply.

📋 Important Note

All policies are designed with your puppy's wellbeing as the top priority. For complete terms and conditions, please contact our support team. We're transparent, fair, and always here to help.
